Automatically selects a usable camera region and colour, then checks distinct beats and waveform stability. Continuously saves clean snippets until loaded measurements are ready. Brief interruptions discard only the unfinished candidate. Saved counts remain visible; Show available report can finish at any time once a result is available.

Current research settings: at least 12 accepted intervals, two nonoverlapping BP segments, and agreement between earlier and later pulse shapes, intervals, amplitudes and widths. These thresholds are provisional, not a validated minimum recording duration.

BP uses original 2.1-second windows inside nonoverlapping 6.3-second segments. The normalized median waveform is display only. RGB is extracted simultaneously from the whole frame and nine fixed regions. One common region is selected for all three colours, then held fixed while snippets accumulate. SpO₂ uses six-second synchronized RGB segments after 4.2 seconds settling. Failed windows are retried one second later; accepted snippets never overlap. Every colour must have repeatable beats, matching pulse rates and stable morphology. Original RGB amplitudes and DC levels enter inference; display normalization does not. Regional RGB has not been validated against the model’s whole-frame training data. More than 5% dark or bright pixels combined in any colour in any frame rejects that RGB segment. Contact, region and sustained exposure changes start a separate group without erasing saved counts. Only compatible snippets within one group are averaged. Inference uses recent snippets (BP: 60 seconds; RGB: 120 seconds); older data expires while session saved counts remain. A single brightness spike does not reset the acquisition. Keep the lens and flash covered; if a colour remains clipped, another rear lens may be needed in Details. These are provisional signal checks, not proof of measurement accuracy.

Trace: baseline removed and auto-scaled; raw RGB is preserved. Waveform appearance does not establish BP accuracy.
