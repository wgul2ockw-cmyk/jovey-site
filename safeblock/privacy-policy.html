SafeBlock privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Applies to SafeBlock for desktop Chrome

Who provides SafeBlock

SafeBlock is published by Jovey. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, contact Mdthitipong@hotmail.com.

Local filtering

SafeBlock uses Chrome’s request-filtering engine with a bundled list of ad and tracker domains. Filtering does not send browsing activity to Jovey, an external DNS provider, or an ad-blocking server. The extension has no telemetry, account system, analytics SDK, remote scripts, or synchronized storage.

Information handled on your device

When you open SafeBlock, temporary active-tab access lets the popup read the current URL to identify its domain and display Chrome’s page count when available. Full URLs, page titles, favicons, and per-page counts are not saved or transmitted. Explicit network exceptions are stored locally and include the selected domain’s subdomains. Cookie-blocking and show-prompt exceptions apply to the exact host you select.

Optional cookie handling reads page elements, layout, and consent labels locally. Essential cookies only tries recognized rejection or necessary-only choices, then hides remaining detected cookie prompts. It never selects Accept all. Hiding a prompt does not prove optional cookies were rejected, and SafeBlock cannot independently classify every cookie. Optional Block all cookies uses Chrome’s cookie settings, can also block essential cookies, and supports user-selected site exceptions. Cookie contents are not read or erased.

Optional YouTube filtering inspects supported player response fields and player elements locally. These page features require website access and run only after you enable them. Page contents, video history, form input, passwords, and authentication cookies are not retained or transmitted. Optional privacy controls read and change supported Chrome privacy-setting values.

Stored information and retention

Chrome stores your feature switches, appearance choice, and explicit website exceptions locally until you change them or remove the extension. Chrome’s native per-page count resets on navigation. From version 1.9, optional session/lifetime totals use Chrome’s matched-rule reporting permission and a periodic alarm, about once a minute. The returned rule ID, ruleset ID, match timestamp, and tab ID are processed in memory to count SafeBlock’s bundled block rules. Individual records and tab IDs are discarded. No request URLs, headers, bodies, or cookie contents are collected by this reporting.

Only aggregate counts, their start/update times, a session identifier, a timestamp/count checkpoint to avoid double counting, and reporting status are retained in extension-local storage. A session token also lives in Chrome session storage. Recorded lifetime totals can be preserved across an upgrade of the same installation. Revoking reporting permission stops polling; recorded totals remain until the extension’s data is removed. Browser interruptions and reporting limits can leave gaps. Counts measure blocked network requests, not unique ads or cookie choices.

No browsing-history log is created. No user data is sold, shared for advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used to determine creditworthiness. The extension sends none of the information described above to Jovey or third parties.

Your controls

Manage network exceptions in Allowed websites and release Chrome privacy overrides in Privacy controls. Choose Essential cookies only, Block all cookies, or Off from the cookie dropdown. Essential and Off clear SafeBlock’s strict cookie rules. Show cookie prompts on this site restores manual consent controls for the selected exact host. Turn YouTube filtering off separately, and manage optional reporting and website permissions in Chrome’s extension settings. Reload affected pages after changes.

Uninstalling SafeBlock removes its extension-local settings and totals through Chrome. Data stored by a separate developer or older QuietBlock installation remains with that installation until it is removed. Browser overrides are released when the controlling extension is removed; previously submitted consent choices remain subject to each website’s cookie/storage rules. Clearing a website’s data is handled in Chrome, not by SafeBlock.

Website, downloads, updates, and support

This website’s interactive preview uses sample state in browser session storage. It is separate from the installed extension and cannot read the extension’s settings. SafeBlock pages bundle their fonts and do not include analytics scripts.

Visiting Jovey.co, downloading files, or following external links makes ordinary web requests to the destination and its hosting provider. This site is hosted using GitHub Pages; hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP addresses. Chrome Web Store installation and updates are handled by Google under its policies. These services are separate from SafeBlock’s local filtering.

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords, private browsing history, or sensitive screenshots. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the contact above, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Changes

Changes to functionality or data handling will be reflected here and, where required, in Chrome’s permission prompts. The effective date will change when this policy changes.
