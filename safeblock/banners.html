Cookie pop-ups

Let SafeBlock choose “Reject all” or “Necessary only” on supported banners.

Automatic rejection

SafeBlock presses a clear rejection button for you. It never chooses “Accept all” or hides a banner without making a choice.

AUTOMATIC REJECTION

Checking…

Removing website access also turns off YouTube filtering, which shares that access.

Allow website access

Chrome asks for permission to read and change web pages so SafeBlock can find rejection controls. Page contents and form values are never saved or sent.

02

Reload your open tabs

Supported banners are handled as pages load. A site may keep a necessary cookie to remember your choice, so it does not ask again.

Coverage, limits & shared access

Supports OneTrust, Cookiebot, CookieYes, Didomi, and clearly labeled rejection buttons in other cookie dialogs. Customized wording, consent paywalls, closed shadow roots, and settings-only banners may need your choice.

SafeBlock makes at most one rejection attempt per page frame in the first 90 seconds. A banner can briefly appear. A dishonest website may ignore the choice. Cookie choices are not added to blocked-request totals.

Turning this feature off removes its website and scripting access, which also disables YouTube filtering. Existing site choices remain. Pausing network blocking does not pause cookie rejection.

Works in desktop Chrome, not dialogs inside other apps or Chrome on iPhone and iPad.
